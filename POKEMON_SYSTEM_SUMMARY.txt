================================================================================
                            STAT SYSTEM
================================================================================

Uses Official Pokémon Gen V+ Formula:

HP: (2*BASE + IV + EV/4) * LEVEL/100 + LEVEL + 10
Other: ((2*BASE + IV + EV/4) * LEVEL/100 + 5) * NATURE_MODIFIER

Components:
- BASE: Species base stat
- IV: Individual value (0-31, randomized per Pokémon)
- EV: Effort value (0-252, trainable)
- LEVEL: Current level (1-100)
- NATURE: One of 25 natures (±10% modifier or neutral)

Stats Calculated:
- HP (Hit Points)
- Attack (Physical damage)
- Defense (Physical defense)
- Special Attack (Special damage)
- Special Defense (Special defense)
- Speed (Action priority)
